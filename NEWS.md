## Heroes

### 1. New Zealand: hung parliament, 65–65
- The Electoral Commission declared the official results of the 7 Nov
  general election on Friday: the ruling centre-right coalition has 65
  seats in the 130-seat parliament — exactly one short of a majority.
- National 40 seats (28.35%, down from 42 on election night), ACT 13,
  NZ First 12. Labour won the party vote, 29.88% (863,238 votes) to
  National's 28.35% (819,012), but took only 36 seats. Greens 17,
  Te Pāti Māori 5.
- The new centrist Opportunity Party won 7 seats and holds the balance
  of power; its 38-year-old leader Qiulae Wong is the kingmaker. Neither
  bloc can govern without her. 2,931,604 votes cast, 484,865 of them
  special votes.
- Sources: Elections NZ (official results), Reuters, AP via LA Post,
  WSJ, 27 Nov.

### 2. Zelensky to the BBC: US "too focused on the Middle East"
- President Zelensky told the BBC the US is too focused on the Middle
  East and not pushing Russia hard enough, and questioned the "strange"
  Trump–Putin friendship: Putin "should not be treated as a friend".
- The same day, Trump said Putin could attend the G20 in Florida on
  14–15 Dec if he wanted ("If he wanted to come, he certainly can"),
  while doubting Putin would want to come; earlier he posted that he
  wants Russia to pull back military aggression in Europe.
- Sources: BBC, Reuters, 27 Nov.

### 6. IAEA seeks ceasefire to fix Zaporizhzhia

### 10. China's supergiant gold haul: ~$300bn
- The Dadonggou deposit in Liaoning holds more than 1,400 tonnes of
  gold (1,444 tonnes proven), the biggest single gold deposit found in
  China since 1949; with Hunan's Wangu field the pair are worth close
  to US$300bn at late-November gold prices (~$3,550/oz). The figure is
  a theoretical gross value, not recoverable value.
- DATE NOTE: the deposit was announced in Nov 2025; the news today is
  the 2026 study in Ore Geology Reviews, reported by ScienceAlert 27 Nov.
- Sources: ScienceAlert, 27 Nov.
